blitsklieg lab

blitsklieg slams shiny extruded 3D type over the web app you already have. This page is its lab: every effect it ships, behind pickers.

Press FIRE in the panel, or hit space anywhere outside it. Shift+Enter in the text box breaks a line; wrap lets long text find its own breaks. An effect is three motion slots — enter, active, exit — plus a material look, and each select lists everything available for that slot. moment plays a canned countdown if you would rather just watch one.

Scroll while it plays: the type holds position, and the page underneath stays readable and clickable through it.
