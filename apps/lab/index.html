blitsklieg lab

blitsklieg slams shiny extruded 3D type over the web app you already have. This page is its lab: every effect it ships, behind pickers.

Press FIRE in a panel, or hit space anywhere outside one. Shift+Enter in the text box breaks a line; wrap lets long text find its own breaks. An effect is three motion slots — enter, active, exit — plus a material look, and each select lists everything available for that slot. moment plays a canned countdown if you would rather just watch one.

Scroll while it plays: the type holds position, and the page underneath stays readable and clickable through it. diagnostics swaps the fired word for a static debug render — colour by pipeline scalar, or a white outline of the glyph the tube follows.
