The point of this page is to be underneath something. If the overlay is compositing correctly you can read every word here through and around the letters, the page scrolls independently, and nothing behind the type is dimmed, boxed, or tinted.

The failure mode this spike exists to catch is a black rectangle. A stock bloom pass writes opaque alpha across the whole frame, so the moment you turn glow on, your transparent overlay stops being transparent and swallows the app. Flip the composite mode in the panel to see it happen.

Keep scrolling — the type is fixed to the viewport, so it should hold position while this text moves past it.

Gold is not a color, it is a reflection. Everything that makes the letters read as metal is coming from a synthetic environment: a dark gradient with a handful of bright bars in it, generated at runtime rather than downloaded. Sliding those bars across the surface is the entire trick.

Turn the environment down to zero and the same material becomes yellow plastic. That is the single most useful thing to see here.
